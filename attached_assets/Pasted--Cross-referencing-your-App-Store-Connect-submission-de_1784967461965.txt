
Cross-referencing your App Store Connect submission details, your App Review Notes, and Apple’s standard Guideline rejection criteria reveals specific misalignments causing these four rejections.

Here is a breakdown of why each violation was flagged based on your metadata and build, along with exact action items to fix them.

1. Guideline 2.1.0 — Performance: App Completeness
Why it failed:
Missing Purchases/Paywall Access: In your App Review Notes, you provided no test credentials for demoing paid features, or your in-app purchase (IAP) products were not attached to the submission.

Incomplete Features or Unhandled Crashes: If reviewers clicked a button (like purchasing, daily wisdom, or voice/web search) and it was non-responsive, hidden, or crashed, it gets flagged under 2.1. (Note: You mentioned fixing an iPad crash caused by expo-glass-effect, but Apple must verify the purchase flow works on live sandbox builds).

What to do:
Submit IAPs with the Build: In App Store Connect, ensure Sirius Plus and Sirius Pro in-app purchases/subscriptions are explicitly added under the In-App Purchases and Subscriptions section of this release.

Provide Reviewer Credentials/Instructions: If your app requires login or has paywalls, tick the Sign-in required checkbox under App Review Information and provide test account credentials, or add explicit reviewer instructions in Notes detailing how to test paid features in Sandbox.

2. Guideline 2.3.10 — Performance: Accurate Metadata
Why it failed:
Promotional Text / Pricing Misrepresentation: In your Promotional Text, you state: Try it free., but under your Description, you specify Free — 30 messages per day alongside paid tiers (£5/mo, £12/mo). Apple rejects metadata if subscription terms, pricing, or "free trial" verbiage are ambiguous or mismatch what is configured in App Store Connect.

Third-Party Platform References or Device Screenshots: If your screenshots show non-iOS frames/UI elements or mention other platforms, Apple rejects under 2.3.10.

What to do:
Clarify Free vs. Trial: If you don't offer an actual StoreKit free trial, change "Try it free." to something unambiguous like "Get started for free." or "Start thinking today."

Synchronize Pricing: Ensure exact prices (e.g., £5/month, £12/month) match the localized StoreKit price tiers set up in App Store Connect.

3. Guideline 3.1.2 — Business: Payments - Subscriptions
Why it failed:
Missing EULA/Terms of Use Link: Auto-renewable subscriptions strictly require a functional link to your Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy in both the App Store Description and inside the app's purchase screen/paywall.   

Missing Auto-Renewable Terms Disclosure: Apple requires explicit subscription disclosures near the paywall button (Title, Billing Period, Price, and how to cancel).   

What to do:
Add Terms of Use (EULA) Link to Description: Add a direct link to your EULA/Terms in the description field (e.g., Apple Standard EULA: [https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/) or your custom terms at [https://sirius-ai.live/terms](https://sirius-ai.live/terms)).

Update In-App Paywall UI: Ensure your in-app subscription screen contains:

Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy links.   

Title, duration, and price of each tier clearly visible before purchase.   

Explicit statement that payment is charged to Apple ID and auto-renews unless canceled 24 hours prior.

4. Guideline 5.1.1 — Legal: Privacy - Data Collection and Storage
Why it failed:
Data Declaration Mismatch: In your notes, you mentioned that "Name was incorrectly categorised as tracking data... The App Privacy information has been corrected." Apple flagged 5.1.1 because your App Privacy disclosures in App Store Connect did not accurately reflect the actual data collected (or requested account data wasn't justified by core features).   

What to do:
Fix App Privacy Section: Go to App Store Connect > App Privacy (on the left menu) and ensure:

Name / User Identifiers are marked under Data Used to Personalize the App or App Functionality, and NOT under Data Used to Track You.

Verify that Privacy Policy URLs inside App Store Connect match [https://sirius-ai.live](https://sirius-ai.live) (or your exact privacy URL).

In-App Privacy Link: Ensure a direct tap to your Privacy Policy is accessible within the app's settings screen and paywall.
