SIRIUS PLATFORM OVERHAUL & CONTENT MARKETING BRIEF
Objective:
To establish a completely unified design and marketing framework for sirius-ai.live that maps the distinct aesthetic of each workspace (Free Utility, The Dream Lab, and The Star Lab) and details the introductory videos needed for each audience tier.
PART 1: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & INTERFACE DESIGN
The platform must physically and visually shift its environment depending on the user's active tier and mindset. The interface must instantly communicate a change in state.
1. The Entry Tier: Free Everyday AI (The Base Layer)
Aesthetic Theme: High-contrast Minimalist / Utilitarian Dark Mode. Deep graphite gray (#18181B) and absolute black background.
The Look & Feel: Sharp, clean lines with minimal distractions. It feels like a high-speed, premium text tool built for frictionless, rapid everyday execution.
Purpose: Replacing ChatGPT for standard daily tasks, 5-minute workflows, and quick conversational needs.
2. The Mid Tier: The Dream Lab (The Intention Space)
Aesthetic Theme: Ethereal, meditative Cloud Theme.
The Look & Feel: Soft, ambient, glowing radial gradients that look like gaseous nebulae or shifting morning clouds. The text styling and borders soften. The color palette pulls in deep midnight blue and calm indigo accents.
Purpose: Broad, blue-sky ideation, vision alignment, manifestation, and mental de-cluttering for seekers, entrepreneurs, and professionals alike.
3. The Premium Tier: The Star Lab (The Architecture Studio)
Aesthetic Theme: Hyper-precise, razor-sharp Star & Constellation Theme.
The Look & Feel: Deep obsidian background with sharp vector constellation networks, single points of intense starlight, and thin laser-accurate gridlines. The color palette uses vibrant electric cyan-blue and emerald-mint green accents to match the primary cinematic artwork.
Purpose: Pure, heavy-duty engineering. Moving from thought to execution instantaneously—where the AI actively codes software, drafts technical frameworks, architectures business plans, and builds operational tools.
PART 2: THE COMPREHENSIVE PLATFORM TOUR BRIEF
The front landing page must host a clean visual player for a Platform Overview Video, split into three distinct, easily scannable segments so users instantly understand the progression:
Segment 1: The Gateway (Everyday Utility) – Demonstrates using the rapid mobile app interface to clear out a 5-minute daily task effortlessly.
Segment 2: Entering The Dream Lab – Shows the interface shifting visually into the calm cloud theme, where an abstract concept (a new department structure, a conscious business concept, or a personal manifestation) is brainstormed and clarified.
Segment 3: Engaging The Star Lab – Shows the final, dramatic visual shift into the constellation environment, demonstrating the AI instantly coding and building a fully realized tool from those exact thoughts in real-time.
