Open your real App Store Connect in a second window/tab. Go section by section, compare every field to what's shown here, and tick the box when it matches exactly.

Why old screenshots may still be showing: App Store Connect stores a separate screenshot set for every display size (6.9"/6.7" iPhone, 6.5" iPhone, 5.5" iPhone, and iPad sizes if your app supports tablets). Deleting screenshots from the main/default view only clears the size bucket you're currently looking at — it does not clear the others. If old screenshots were uploaded to more than one size bucket at any point, they can persist in a bucket you never revisited, and Apple's reviewer or automated scan can pull from any of them. Use the checklist below to check every bucket, not just the default one.
